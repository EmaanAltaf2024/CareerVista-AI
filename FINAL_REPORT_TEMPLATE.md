## 1. Introduction
CareerVista AI is an educational prototype designed to help students and fresh graduates explore job roles that may align with their resume skills. It combines text processing, a machine-learning category classifier, similarity-based job ranking, and a Streamlit interface.

## 2. Problem statement
Students and fresh graduates may struggle to translate their education, projects, and skills into suitable job-role options. The system provides an initial comparison between resume text and a small role dataset.

## 3. Objectives
- Preprocess job and resume text.
- Demonstrate an ML model for job-category prediction.
- Rank job records using text similarity and required-skill overlap.
- Demonstrate a neural network in the companion notebook.
- Visualize dataset records and expose an interactive interface.
- Help users identify skills listed by matching roles.

## 4. Dataset and limitations
The supplied starter CSV contains 40 curated prototype records and 32 categories. It is not a representative sample of the Pakistani job market and does not represent live vacancies. Record-count charts describe this dataset only, not market demand. Add verified, licensed, source-attributed job listings before making market-wide claims.

## 6. Models and recommendation method
- TF-IDF converts text into weighted word/ngram features.
- LinearSVC is used for category prediction when labels have enough examples.
- TF-IDF cosine similarity ranks job descriptions against resume text.
- Required-skill phrase overlap is combined with similarity to produce a ranking score.
- The Colab notebook demonstrates an MLPClassifier neural network.

The match score is not a hiring probability. Record the actual model metrics generated by your run; do not invent accuracy values.
